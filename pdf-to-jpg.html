What is pdf to jpg?

PDF-to-JPG conversion renders each document page as a JPEG image. PDFora creates one JPG per page and packages multi-page results in a ZIP archive.

How to use PDF to JPG

  1. 1

    Upload a PDF.

  2. 2

    Choose 96, 150, or 200 DPI depending on the detail you need.

  3. 3

    Start conversion and wait for all pages to render.

  4. 4

    Download the ZIP and extract the JPG files.

Why use this tool?

JPG is broadly supported by websites, presentation software, messaging tools, and image editors. It is often a practical choice for photographic pages or quick visual previews where a PDF file is not accepted.

How PDFora works

PDFora validates the selected files and options, processes the task locally inside your browser, and creates the download on your device. Document contents are not sent to PDFora servers.

Common ways to use PDF to JPG

  • Create image previews for a document
  • Use PDF pages in software that accepts JPG but not PDF
  • Share individual visual pages
  • Prepare page images for presentations or web content

Tips for better results

  • Use 96 DPI when smaller images matter most.
  • 150 DPI is a good general-purpose setting.
  • Use 200 DPI for sharper text and graphics, but expect a larger ZIP.
  • Use PDF to PNG instead when crisp diagrams, screenshots, or text edges matter more than file size.

Frequently asked questions

Will a multi-page PDF work?

Yes. Every page is exported as a separate JPG inside the ZIP archive.

Which resolution should I choose?

150 DPI is a practical default. Higher DPI produces larger, sharper images.

Why does JPG not preserve transparency?

PDF pages are rendered onto an opaque page background before JPEG encoding because JPEG has no alpha-transparency channel.

Will links or selectable text remain interactive?

No. JPG output is a raster image, so PDF text selection, links, forms, and other interactive features are not preserved.
