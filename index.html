Practical PDF guidance

Choose the right workflow before you upload.

Different PDF tasks solve different problems. These quick explanations help you pick the simplest tool and avoid unnecessary conversions.

01

Reduce a file without rebuilding it

Use Compress PDF when your document is too large for email, a portal, or convenient sharing. Compression works best on PDFs with large embedded images; already-optimized documents may change very little.

Compress PDF →
02

Combine or reorganize existing pages

Use Merge PDF to join separate documents, Reorder PDF Pages to fix sequence, and Extract or Delete PDF Pages when you need only part of a larger file. These tools preserve PDF pages instead of turning them into images.

Organize PDF pages →
03

Convert only when another format is needed

PDF to JPG or PNG is useful when a website or creative workflow needs images. JPG is usually smaller for photographic pages, while PNG often keeps diagrams, screenshots, and sharp edges cleaner.

FAQ

Useful answers before you upload.

PDFora is intentionally straightforward, but these details help set clear expectations.

Do I need to install software?

No. PDFora runs in a modern browser. The current PDF engine processes files locally on your device.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the core tools in this version.

How large can my files be?

The current interface accepts files up to 50 MB each. Processing speed and practical capacity depend on your browser and device memory.

Are my documents visible to other users?

No. The platform does not provide a public document list. PDF processing happens locally in your browser, and document contents are not uploaded to PDFora servers.
