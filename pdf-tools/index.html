Before you process

Keep the original document.

PDFora creates new output files rather than acting as permanent cloud storage. Keep your original locally, review every result, and use the downloaded copy only after confirming its pages and visual quality are correct.

File privacy

Local by design.

Selected files are handled locally in your browser. The processed result is created on your device, and document contents are not sent to PDFora servers or used as permanent document storage.
