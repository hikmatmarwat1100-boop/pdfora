What is delete pdf pages?

Deleting PDF pages creates a new document that excludes the page numbers you select. The pages you keep remain in their original sequence.

How to use Delete PDF Pages

  1. 1

    Upload the PDF.

  2. 2

    Enter the page numbers or ranges you want to remove.

  3. 3

    Start processing; PDFora verifies that at least one page will remain.

  4. 4

    Download the new PDF and confirm the correct pages were removed.

Why use this tool?

Removing blank scans, outdated inserts, accidental duplicates, or irrelevant sections can make a document smaller, clearer, and safer to share when those pages are not needed.

How PDFora works

PDFora validates the selected files and options, processes the task locally inside your browser, and creates the download on your device. Document contents are not sent to PDFora servers.

Common ways to use Delete PDF Pages

  • Remove blank scanner pages
  • Delete obsolete pages before sharing
  • Remove duplicate pages from a packet
  • Create a cleaner version of a longer report

Tips for better results

  • Page ranges can be entered as 2,4-6,9.
  • Use Extract PDF Pages when it is easier to specify the pages you want to keep.
  • The tool prevents deleting every page from the document.
  • Keep your original file until you have checked the new version.

Frequently asked questions

How do I enter a page range?

Use commas and hyphens, for example 2,4-6,9.

Can I delete every page?

No. At least one page must remain in the output PDF.

Does the page numbering start at 1?

Yes. Enter page numbers the way you normally see them: 1 for the first PDF page, 2 for the second, and so on.

Will my original PDF be changed?

No. PDFora creates a separate output document.
