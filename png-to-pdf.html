What is png to pdf?

PNG-to-PDF conversion creates PDF pages from PNG images and combines multiple images into one document. Transparent PNG regions are placed on a white background so the resulting PDF pages have predictable appearance.

How to use PNG to PDF

  1. 1

    Choose one or more PNG images.

  2. 2

    Arrange the image list in the desired page order.

  3. 3

    Start conversion.

  4. 4

    Download the generated PDF and review each page.

Why use this tool?

PNG is common for screenshots, diagrams, exported graphics, and digital documents. Combining these images into one PDF can make them easier to print, submit, archive, or share as a single file.

How PDFora works

PDFora validates the selected files and options, processes the task locally inside your browser, and creates the download on your device. Document contents are not sent to PDFora servers.

Common ways to use PNG to PDF

  • Combine screenshots into a report
  • Create a document from diagrams
  • Package exported graphics for printing
  • Turn several PNG scans into one PDF

Tips for better results

  • Set the page order before processing.
  • Remember that transparent pixels become white in the PDF.
  • Very large screenshots can produce large PDF files; compress the result if needed.
  • Use JPG to PDF when your source files are JPEG images.

Frequently asked questions

How is PNG transparency handled?

Transparent pixels are flattened onto a white background when the PDF is created.

Can I combine multiple PNG files?

Yes. Selected images are combined into one PDF in the displayed order.

Can I reorder images on a phone?

Yes. Use the move-up and move-down buttons in the selected-file list.

Does conversion make a PNG editable as PDF text?

No. The image remains image-based content inside the PDF.
